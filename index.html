    Why SmogWise requests your location

    We use your approximate browser geolocation solely to query the nearest official ground monitoring station (via WAQI) and local weather metrics (via Open-Meteo).

    • 🔒 Private: Your GPS coordinates remain in your browser session and are never logged on our servers or sold.
    • 🎯 Station proximity: Stations are typically within 2–15 km of your location.
    • 🚫 Permission denial: If you choose not to share, you can easily type any city name in the search box.

    Air Quality & Pollutants Guide

    1. The US EPA AQI Scale

    The Air Quality Index (AQI) converts complex pollutant concentrations into a simple 0 to 500 scale. The higher the AQI value, the greater the level of air pollution and the greater the health concern.

    AQI Range Category Meaning & Action
    0 – 50 Good Air quality is satisfactory; air pollution poses little or no risk.
    51 – 100 Moderate Acceptable quality; unusually sensitive people should consider reducing exertion.
    101 – 150 Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups Children, older adults, and individuals with respiratory issues should limit prolonged exertion.
    151 – 200 Unhealthy Everyone may begin to experience health effects; sensitive groups more severe.
    201 – 300 Very Unhealthy Health alert: increased risk of health effects for entire population.
    301 – 500 Hazardous Health warning of emergency conditions. Entire population is affected.

    3. Note on India National AQI (CPCB)

    🇮🇳
    WAQI uses the US EPA AQI standard to normalize international measurements. India’s Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) operates its own National Air Quality Index (NAQI) with 6 bands: Good (0-50), Satisfactory (51-100), Moderately Polluted (101-200), Poor (201-300), Very Poor (301-400), and Severe (401-500). Because the 24-hour concentration breakpoints differ, a US AQI value of 150 is not identical to an Indian NAQI score of 150.
